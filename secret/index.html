How to interpret this survey
Using tags
Choose Setting, Structure, Guidance and Data requirements. Setting and Structure are independent: a procedural task can be physical or virtual. All selected requirements must be supported in the same dataset. The draft labels are operational definitions, not final project claims. Procedural Task requires predefined or generated steps throughout the task; a final target or post-hoc action labels alone do not establish it. Steps may branch or change during execution. Non-procedural Task covers documented goal-directed or continuous/reactive activities without a task-wide step plan; unclear evidence remains unverified. A mixed dataset may have both Structure labels for different activities. Selecting both finds datasets covering both types, not one activity with contradictory structures. Subset coverage is marked beside the Structure labels. Live guidance, instruction materials and offline guidance benchmarks are distinguished. Open Details for the evidence behind each label.
Compare like with like
Some durations sum multiple camera views; others count task sessions. Derived datasets can reuse footage and participants; do not sum their totals. A > marker is a lower bound. Participant counts can refer to collected, analyzed, or released cohorts. Each entry explains its basis.
Missing data and pupil signals
N/A marks an absent or inapplicable measure/stream, or an unavailable numeric value/link. Not reported and release unverified remain explicit when the evidence is uncertain. Other-state measures are retained in Details. Camera frame rates are not necessarily gaze rates; native and derived pupil measurements are distinguished.
Scope of the conclusions
These 34 dataset and benchmark entries support screening and comparison. System-focused and analysis-focused papers are excluded from this view. They do not establish that no other datasets exist. No full data archives were downloaded. Unresolved access, signal quality, or protocol details are recorded for each paper.
Corrections to the synthesis
COLET is not the only study to control lighting: IMPACT, IndustReal, and IREYE4TASK also describe lighting controls. Assembly101 provides a completed-toy image. Ego-EXTRA’s protocol order is not a counterbalanced comparison.
Original entries are preserved
Open a paper to read its corrected entry, review notes, primary sources, and original PDF wording where available. New entries identify their primary sources. The original is kept separately and may contain claims corrected by the review.
